Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 13, 2026

Garage Hero LLC, a North Carolina limited liability company ("Garage Hero," "we," "us"), operates the Garage Hero mobile application, a marketplace connecting homeowners with vetted service providers ("Heroes") for garage cleanout, junk removal, and organization services. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email address, phone number, and password (stored as a secure hash).
  • Job information: service address, city, state, ZIP, scheduling preferences, job descriptions, and photos of your space that you choose to upload.
  • Garage measurements: dimensions you enter (or capture with device scanning features) to receive accurate quotes.
  • Payment information: payments are processed by Stripe. We never store your full card number; Stripe provides us only a secure payment token and transaction status.
  • Provider information (Heroes): application details, service areas, and background-check status.

How we use information

  • To create and manage your account and match homeowners with providers.
  • To generate quotes, schedule jobs, process payments, and pay providers.
  • To send service notifications (job status, messages) by push notification or SMS.
  • To keep the marketplace safe, prevent fraud, and comply with law.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your information for third-party advertising.

Sharing

Job details (first name, address, photos, measurements) are shared with the Hero who accepts your job — that's how the service works. We use trusted processors to run the app: Supabase (database and authentication), Stripe (payments), and Twilio (SMS). Each processes data only on our instructions.

Your choices

  • Access and update: edit your profile any time in the app.
  • Delete your account: Profile → Account settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your profile, jobs, measurements, and sign-in. Transaction records required for tax or legal purposes may be retained by our payment processor.
  • Notifications: disable push notifications in your device settings.

Data retention & security

We keep your data while your account is active. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest by our infrastructure providers. Access is limited by row-level security so users can only read the records that belong to them.

Children

Garage Hero is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We'll post any changes to this policy here and update the effective date.
